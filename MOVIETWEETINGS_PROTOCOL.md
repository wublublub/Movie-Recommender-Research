# MovieTweetings protocol lock (2026-10-02)

The primary positive event is a rating of at least **8/10**. This was specified in the integrated MovieTweetings runner before any local pilot result was observed. It represents a high-positive rating on the source scale; it is not assumed equivalent to MovieLens's 4/5 threshold. Ratings at least 7 and at least 9 remain separate, later robustness checks. The eligible population is defined by the existing chronological 5 training, 2 validation, and 2 test minimum, and excludes users with shorter positive histories.

The pinned source is `sidooms/MovieTweetings` commit `44c525d0c766944910686c60697203cda39305d6`. Raw ratings remain unchanged. Numeric MovieID normalization removes leading zeroes for in-memory joins and ranking. The catalog is the sorted unique set of normalized metadata IDs (38,013 items), including cold items. Five pairs of raw metadata IDs map to the same numeric ID: `000000000106519`/`0106519` to 106519, `01361336`/`1361336` to 1361336, `01979376`/`1979376` to 1979376, `04160708`/`4160708` to 4160708, and `0009620292`/`9620292` to 9620292. All five have rated events; no events are dropped. Both aliases remain in the raw files and audit output. Titles and genres are not used for ranking. The primary scope is the source movie-rating catalog, which includes shorts and has no independently verified feature-film-only filter.

The formal comparison uses Original and Fully Shuffled pre-test positive histories, one fixed chronological split, identical users, held-out targets, unseen-item candidate sets, and interaction multisets. Tuning uses Original-order validation only. FPMC uses 24 factors, 3 epochs, learning rate 0.03, regularization 0.002, and negative-sampling seed 17760, selecting transition weight from 0.5 and 1.0. LSTM uses 32-dimensional embeddings and hidden state, maximum length 64, learning rate 0.001, and an Original-validation-selected epoch within five. SASRec selects between `(32, 50, 1 block, 2 heads, 0.1 dropout, 0.001 learning rate)` and `(64, 100, 2 blocks, 4 heads, 0.2 dropout, 0.0005 learning rate)`, with an Original-validation-selected epoch within five. Selected settings are frozen across order conditions. The runner's current formal default has initialization seed 760 and shuffle seeds 20760, 20761, and 20762.

The bounded pilot command uses the same threshold, catalog, split, tuning grid, and five-epoch cap as the formal runner. It selects 400 eligible users deterministically, one shuffle seed, and only FPMC, LSTM, and SASRec. It omits static baselines and the secondary recent-context intervention to measure the cost of the primary comparison. Pilot effects are diagnostic and must not be interpreted as scientific estimates.

## Medium-pilot decision

The 400-user pilot used 9,224 training, 1,260 validation, and 1,260 test positive events. Its two independent invocations produced byte-identical splits, per-user metrics, selected settings, aggregate scores, and order effects. The completed invocation is `outputs/movietweetings_experiment/medium_pilot_u400_20261002_v2/`; the first invocation remains preserved, but its completion metadata failed because Git rejected the sandbox's repository ownership. All six model/condition cells completed in both invocations. The completed invocation took 72.7 seconds, peaked at 1.40 GiB process RSS, and wrote 42.1 MB. The order audit found zero multiset or candidate/target mismatches.

The pilot does **not** qualify as a formal result. Original/Fully Shuffled test NDCG@10 was 0/0 for FPMC, 0.014372/0.019044 for LSTM, and 0.011531/0.011345 for SASRec. FPMC's selected Original-validation score was nonzero (0.000684), but it had no test hit in either condition. Both neural losses declined over five epochs and their Original-validation NDCG increased through epoch five; no nonfinite scores or failed training cells were observed. Neither the negative LSTM pilot delta nor the zero FPMC test score is grounds for post-hoc threshold or objective tuning.

For cross-dataset comparability and to capture initialization sensitivity, the formal design used **five initialization seeds (760–764) by three shuffle seeds (20760–20762)**, with Original-validation-only selection for each initialization and settings frozen across order conditions. The completed formal run is archived at `outputs/movietweetings_experiment/formal_rating8_5init_20261002/`; its `completion.json`, `protocol.json`, split assignments, per-user metrics, and integrity audit are the authoritative run evidence. The earlier 1,500-user diagnostic produced nonzero FPMC test hits without changing its objective or settings; see `MOVIETWEETINGS_FPMC_DIAGNOSTIC.md` for that bounded pilot context.
